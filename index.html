Privacy Policy for Daily Calculator

Package Name: com.aistudio.dailycalculator.calcik • Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Daily Calculator is an offline-first utility and personal finance calculator app designed with user privacy as a core principle.

1. Data Collection & Sharing

Daily Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal, financial, or usage data. All calculations run 100% locally on your device.

2. Local Storage

Your saved calculation history, favorite tools, and app preferences (theme, language, and haptic feedback settings) are stored exclusively on your own device using Android Room Database and Jetpack DataStore. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data permanently removes all locally stored records.

3. Permissions & Internet Access

Daily Calculator does not require account registration or login and does not request sensitive device permissions (such as location, camera, contacts, microphone, or media storage).

4. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

Daily Calculator contains no third-party advertisements and no behavioral tracking SDKs.

5. Financial Disclaimer

Financial results (EMI, SIP, FD/RD, PPF, GST, Compound Interest, Loan, and Salary estimates) are provided strictly for informational and planning purposes. Actual figures from banks, tax authorities, or financial institutions may vary.
